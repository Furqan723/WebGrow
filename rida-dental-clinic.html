About the clinic

A clinic that explains before it treats

Rida Dental Clinic sits on Airport Road opposite Askari 10, in a space built around calm: marble walls, walnut cabinetry, and treatment rooms kept spotless between every patient. It's the kind of detail patients mention on their own — clean, organized, unhurried.

What comes up most in patient feedback isn't a single procedure — it's the way each one is explained. Before a filling, an extraction or a full orthodontic plan, you hear the options, the trade-offs, and what to expect, in plain language, before anything starts.

What we treat

Everyday dentistry to full orthodontic care

Eleven core services, from a routine check-up to a complete implant or braces plan.

Teeth whitening

Brighten discoloured or stained teeth in a single in-clinic session.

Root canal (Rotary RCT)

Rotary-instrument root canal treatment for infected or badly decayed teeth.

Restoration

Composite, GIC, amalgam and cermet fillings matched to the tooth and the damage.

Scaling & polishing

Routine deep cleaning to clear plaque, tartar and surface staining.

Crowns & bridges

Including zirconium crowns, for teeth that are broken, weak or missing.

Orthodontics (braces)

Metal, clear/invisible braces and Invisalign-style aligners for alignment and bite correction.

Dental implants

Permanent, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth.

Tooth extraction

Including surgical removal of wisdom teeth, done under local anaesthesia.

Dentures

Complete and partial dentures, fitted and adjusted for comfort.

Retainers

Fixed, Hawley's and Essix vacuum-formed retainers to hold results after braces.

Checkups & X-rays

General checkups and dental X-rays to catch problems early.
